Character Creation
Lets you build a custom hero from scratch and start a new game in Luka's place. You choose your hero's name, looks, sex, starting job, race, equipment, and a custom trait. The story still treats your hero as Luka, but they bring their own identity and abilities.

Note: This mod replaces Luka Replacer. If you have Luka Replacer installed, remove it first. This mod uses its own compatibility layer (MGQ_LukaReplacer) to talk to the Multiplayer mod.

What changes
After New Game, a menu lets you create a new character, load an existing .luka hero file, delete a hero, or start as default Luka.

Your Hero: Your name, sprite, face, and picture replace Luka's on the map, in menus, in battle, and in story messages. Story pictures still show Luka.

Battle Lines: In battle, your hero speaks the lines of the character they come from, with their own face, expressions, and cut-ins.

Traits: Your hero grows by their own base stats and has their own custom trait instead of Hero's Blood. They keep Luka's Talk skill, which recruiting needs.

Story Changes: Where the story gives Luka a job, race, skill, or change of form, your hero gets it, nothing, or something else, as their hero file says.

Forms: In Luka's Part 3 forms, your hero can look different and speak other lines; Father of Chaos, World Breaker, and Judgement stay Luka.

Sex: Determines which skills enemy use on you. Male-target skills on males, and Female-target skills on females.

How to Use
Start a New Game: Select "Create New Character" from the title screen menu.

Import Images: Click "Import" to select a folder from Patch/Character_Creation/Imports/. A valid folder must contain at least face.png and sprite.png. You can also include picture.png and cutin.png. Refer to the cecil folder as an example.

Customize: Edit your name, artist, sex, bust size, starting job, and starting race.

Trait: Click "Trait" to spend your Trait Points (max 100). You can mix and match features like stat boosts, elemental resistances, skill types (e.g., "Can always use Sword skills"), and more.

Cut-ins: If your import folder has a cutin.png, you can assign it to a skill type (e.g., Sword, Sexcraft). Whenever your hero uses a skill of that type, the cut-in plays.

Save: Click "Save" to save your hero. They will appear in the Patch/Character_Creation/Heroes/ folder as a .luka file, and you will start the game.

Multiplayer
With Monster Girl Quest! Online, other players see your hero in battles and on the map. The mod automatically shares your .luka file with other players in the world. Players without this mod, or without your hero's file, will see Luka.

Installation
Install the community's mod loader: the Patch.rb (enable Type 1 mods) on the MGQ wiki into your Patch folder. If you already use other Patch folder mods, you have it.

Extract the Character_Creation.zip into the game folder, the one with Game.exe. Its Patch folder puts Character_Creation.rb into your Patch folder and the scripts into Patch\Character_Creation\Scripts.

If you are upgrading from Luka Replacer, delete Patch\Luka_Replacer.rb and the Patch\Luka_Replacer folder.

Uninstalling
To uninstall, delete Patch\Character_Creation.rb and the Patch\Character_Creation folder. A save started with a custom hero keeps their starting job and gear; to play as Luka again, start a new game.
